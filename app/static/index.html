RESTLESS BANDIT EQUATION

Bayesian CTMC Belief Decay

$$p(t + \Delta t) = \pi + (p(t) - \pi) \cdot e^{-\Lambda \cdot \Delta t}$$

Channels turn on and off whether we observe them or not. As time since the last visit grows, belief decays toward the stationary prior $\pi$. Channels observed empty also decay upward back to prior.

DECISION CRITERIA

Reward-Rate Optimization Index

$$r(t) = \sum_p \left[ w_p \cdot \text{detected}(p,t) \right] - L_d \cdot t_{\text{dwell}} - L_f \cdot |\Delta f| - L_0$$

Because $L_f > 0$, frequency thrashing is penalised and nearby channels are grouped. Because $L_0 > 0$, doing nothing (sleep) is optimal when all scan candidates have negative expected return.
